# Lessons

- In itinerary results, remove repeated destination photography unless each image adds day-specific information.
- Make recommendations concrete with a named shop, venue, neighborhood, or landmark, and reuse the planner's interest taxonomy to explain why it fits.
- Treat the route map as a core editorial visualization, not a rough placeholder.
- Align visual sets deliberately, place range labels at their true numeric positions, and never restate an activity title as its recommendation; add a concrete venue and what to order or notice.
